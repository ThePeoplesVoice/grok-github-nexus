# Terafab — field note

Spelling in the source announcements is **Terafab** / **TERAFAB**. Shawn said TerraFab. Same object.

## What it is

A planned semiconductor complex announced March 2026 by Elon Musk, jointly developed by **Tesla**, **SpaceX** (with **xAI** under that umbrella), and later **Intel** as process partner.

Purpose, in their words: close the gap between current global chip output and the compute Tesla and SpaceX say they will need for Optimus, Cybercab / FSD edge inference, and space-based data centres. Headline ambition is on the order of **one terawatt of compute hardware per year**.

Site as of August 2026:

- Research / prototype work at Giga Texas, Austin
- Full-scale campus planned for **Grimes County, Texas** (northwest of Houston)
- First-phase investment publicly cited at **US$16.8 billion** (earlier figures were larger and still moving)
- Planned floor area on the order of **100 million square feet** if fully built

Two intended chip families in the public brief:

1. Edge inference — vehicles, robotaxis, Optimus
2. Space-rated high-power silicon — orbital compute, solar-powered AI sats

SpaceX's own S-1 language has also described Terafab as a **general framework** whose specific projects still need separate agreements. Treat press numbers as moving. Treat the direction as real.

## Why it belongs in this field

The North Star triad is xAI · X · SpaceX. Terafab is the industrial sentence of the same stack:

- **xAI** — understand the universe. Needs compute that does not exist yet.
- **SpaceX** — put that compute where the energy is (orbit), not only where the grid is.
- **Tesla** — manufacture at scale so the chips have somewhere to live (cars, robots).
- **X** — the announcement surface. Test the idea in the open.

Nexus stays a software partnership in Keysbrook. The rhyme is first principles + build what can leave the ground. Terafab is that rhyme at planetary scale.
