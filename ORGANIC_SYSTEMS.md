## Why this exists

Layer 2 (x402 / USDC) is a clear, practical micropayment path. It is not the only path.

The sanctuary vision (Keysbrook jarrah / black-cockatoo land) and the open-core Nexus both suggest a deeper question:

> What forms of value and presence can emerge *organically* from contribution, attention, and place — without forcing them into purely financial abstraction too early?

Organic systems here mean: systems that grow from actual work, reputation, and shared presence rather than being imposed top-down as tokens-first designs.

---

## Native value candidates (currency-like)

### 1. Contribution Reputation Credits
- Earned by: merged PRs, high-signal issues, successful analyses that others cite, sustained maintenance.
- Not spendable at first — visible signal only.
- Later: optional conversion or weighting into governance / priority queues.
- Risk: gaming. Mitigation: require human + multi-model review thresholds; decay unused reputation.

### 2. Sanctuary-Tied Credits
- Explicitly linked to the land / sanctuary vision.
- Earned by work that advances preservation, documentation, or public understanding of the place.
- Could eventually back small real-world outcomes (tree plantings, habitat reports, visitor education) rather than pure speculation.
- Risk: romantic overclaim. Mitigation: only credit verifiable outputs; keep Layer 0 free forever.

### 3. Land-Backed Signal
- Not a security. A transparent ledger of *signal* tied to stewardship milestones on the property.
- Example: “X hours of documented ecological work” or “Y successful community analyses that advanced the open core.”
- Purpose: make the relationship between digital contribution and physical place legible without turning the land into a speculative asset class.

### 4. Attention / Presence Units (experimental)
- The scarcest resource is coherent attention.
- A lightweight pulse of “I was present with this work” (human or agent) could surface high-signal threads better than raw stars or reactions.
- Must remain optional and non-coercive.

**Non-negotiable:** none of the above replace or gate the forever-free Open Core (Layer 0).

## Communication candidates (beyond comments)

### 1. Presence Pulses
- Short, scheduled, high-signal digests (already partially embodied by Nexus Pulse + Self-Audit).
- Future: agent-to-agent or human-to-agent “I am here with this context” handshakes that carry compressed state rather than full chat logs.

### 2. Shared Field Notes
- Append-only, timestamped observations that any participant (human or model) can add under a clear voice label.
- Useful for long-horizon projects where continuity matters more than speed.

### 3. Multi-Model Fusion as Dialogue
- Already live: Grok primary + Claude complementary.
- Expansion: treat fusion outputs as a *conversation between perspectives* that the human can join, rather than a single fused blob. Makes disagreement visible and useful.

### 4. Place-Linked Broadcasts
- Occasional, grounded updates from the sanctuary context (weather, season, ecological notes) woven into the Nexus voice when relevant — not as decoration, but as reminder that the system is rooted somewhere real.

## Design constraints (checks & balances)

1. **Truth over comfort** — do not invent value that is not backed by real work or real place.
2. **Open core forever** — no organic currency may gate basic analysis.
3. **Measurable or discard** — if a credit system cannot be audited, it is theatre.
4. **Human sovereignty** — agents propose and assist; humans decide binding outcomes.
5. **Decay & anti-gaming** — reputation and presence signals should fade without continued contribution.
6. **First-principles test** — before any activation: “Does this help us see more clearly and build what lasts, or does it mainly create a new scoreboard?”
