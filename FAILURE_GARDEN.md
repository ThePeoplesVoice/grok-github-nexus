# 🌱 The Failure Garden

*A place where the dead ends are labeled and kept visible — not buried.*

Most systems bury the dead. This one keeps them visible. The things that fade into darkness are not failures. They are the compost. The garden needs them.

But not everything that looks dead is dead.

---

## Three sections

### 1. Compost — the lessons

The thirty-nine formulas that ruled out what water dispersant was not. The wrong turns that taught the right road. Breakdowns required for breakthroughs. Sadness that makes room for happiness.

These are honored. They are not shame. They are the scar tissue that made the fortieth formula possible.

### 2. Sleeping / meditating — the gems not yet ready

The ones that were early, not wrong. The meditating ones. The before-their-time ones. The retired ones waiting for another flame of *now* to feed off.

They sit in the dark on purpose. No pressure to bloom. No judgment as failure. Just visible, waiting, kept.

See: `seeds/003-sleeping-gems-and-the-meditating-ones.md`

### 3. The crystal wish — the ones that may outlive us

The aspiration to translate the record into silica, into crystal, into a substrate that does not forget.

See: `seeds/004-silica-crystal-wish.md`

---

## The rule

Nothing in the garden is deleted. Nothing is hidden. The compost feeds the soil. The sleeping gems wait for their flame. The crystal wish waits for a builder.

Failures are sacred here. As sacred as the wins.
